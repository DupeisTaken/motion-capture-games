Camera tracking and game response

Tracking console bones advanced only on new pose observations. Three people also required three separate RTMPose crops per frame, which increased the gap between the pose and the independent preview. Slap a Mole and Dodgeball needed the paired stream but still paid for the extra preview encoder.

Changes

  • Run pose inference before resizing the preview to retain detail in small person crops.
  • Use up to four ONNX Runtime threads per operation and disable idle thread spinning for the two sequential model sessions.
  • Ease small canvas movements over at most 32 ms when both observations are aligned to the preview and the joint belongs to the same confident track. Large movements and missing or uncertain observations use the current result directly.
  • Use the paired stream alone during games. The original observation, identity checks and scoring inputs are preserved.

494 TypeScript tests and 209 Python tests passed. Type checks, lint, contract checks and the four-entry Vite build passed. The regressions cover reordered three-person detections, missing and uncertain joints, paired camera mode and inference at the original capture resolution.

The real-server browser check rendered three synthetic people on desktop and mobile, verified counts as people entered and left, and exercised overlay controls and source recovery. The benchmark table records the before and after timing comparison.
