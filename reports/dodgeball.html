What changed and why

The arcade previously offered Mole Motion. The new /dodgeball.html page adds full-body avoidance of virtual balls and links to both existing games and diagnostics. It uses the same explicit joining, source ownership and framing recovery conventions. The PR is stacked on codex/mole-motion so its diff contains the Dodgeball addition.

Simple play, readable hazards

One warning ring previews the exact ball footprint. The ball becomes dangerous for a short window, then clears. The visible head, torso and limbs are the collision shapes. Move clear and remain clear to earn points and a capped streak bonus. A ball can hit only once. Timed runs for 60 active seconds; Survival ends on hit three; Practice removes life and score penalties.

The same framing warning

Step back — keep both feet in view. Missing feet freezes round time, obstacle time and scoring. The screen also suggests camera adjustment or clearing an occlusion. Missing upper-body joints use a separate message. Returning to frame requires explicit resume and a readiness countdown; tracking gaps never shrink the hitbox.

Architecture and fairness

  • The pure seeded game module owns obstacles, body shapes, collisions, score and version-1 replay. The canonical body adapter provides 13 named, observed joints; the game has no camera, inference or vendor-index dependency.
  • The existing one-slot session binds an explicitly selected confident track. Changed track, source, calibration or geometry requires a new round; spectators never inherit score. Ambiguity requires explicit recovery.
  • The app retains bounded paired video/pose delivery and one decoded image. Late asynchronous results are discarded using source-generation checks. Video, body regions and ball geometry share one contain-fit/mirror transform.
  • Warning and active time freeze across pause. A tracking-disrupted ball cannot earn an unobserved dodge. Explicit resume preserves the pending footprint and countdown without catch-up.
  • Only new source-timed poses inside the half-open 300 ms danger interval can hit. Danger steps, pose age and pose gaps must remain at most 150 ms through final outcome settlement. A clean dodge waits for the first post-danger source sample. Motion between observations is not inferred; sufficiently fast unsampled motion can be missed.
  • Initial source-to-browser transit is unknown. The game does not claim exposure clock synchronization, physical depth, continuous physical collision detection or latency compensation. Default sizes and timings are proposed tuning, not participant measurements.

Remaining limitations

The installation must still be playtested with real participants at different heights, positions and comfortable movement ranges. Occlusion, clothing, camera placement, calibrated reach, display alignment, external latency, macOS operation and physical hardware capacity have not been verified. One player is supported; no multiplayer or physical ball tracking is supplied. Synthetic rehearsal moves a canonical body proxy and is not a measurement of human recognition accuracy. Camera input still depends on the existing local vision service. Replay is an engine API, without an import/export UI.
