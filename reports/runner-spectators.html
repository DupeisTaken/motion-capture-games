Motion Runner continues when spectators enter

November 11, 2026

The previous runner paused as soon as a second person appeared, even while the player remained clearly tracked. The pose adapter now reads the uniquely identified track bound to the run. The engine advances countdown, obstacles, time, distance and score while that track supplies fresh, valid input. Camera setup still begins with one person in view.

If the joined player disappears, becomes ambiguous or loses required tracking, the run pauses. Another person's track cannot inherit the controls or the score.

Runner issue #39 · Runner PR #44

Verification

  • pnpm check passed in the combined preview, including 718 TypeScript tests, 208 Python tests, contract checks, static analysis and formatting.
  • pnpm build built all six preview entries and verified their asset references. Vite reported the existing advisory for the roughly 549 kB minified Runner bundle.
  • pnpm benchmark:runner passed 48 synthetic cases across both worlds, both paces, four seeds and three input rates. There were zero oracle hits. Spectator entry preserved play and owner loss froze distance, score and time exactly.
  • Camera packet tests used synthetic canonical joints. The player continued to steer and score even when the spectator was listed first, and replacement tracks could not resume the run.
  • Chrome tested the live Vite pages in English at 1440 × 1050 and 390 × 844 with zero page errors. Before the change, selecting the spectator paused the run. After the change, the HUD advanced from 2 to 10 m on desktop and 2 to 10 m on mobile with the spectator selected. Selecting lost tracking while the spectator remained present froze distance at both sizes.
