Skeleton diagnostics

A local mirror that draws body joints and automatically counts current people detections. The same named joints form the input boundary for future games.

What shipped

Choose a camera or a generated scene. The camera path offers MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite and RTMDet-nano with RTMPose-m on CPU. Box overlap and pose-assisted association are selectable independently. The count comes from current detections. When a detector reaches its configured limit, the viewer indicates that more people may be in view.

Vision owns acquisition, inference and diagnostic IDs. The browser draws joints and the image from the same bounded binary packet. Both use one aspect-preserving presentation transform and preserve anatomical left and right. Each preview advances at inference cadence. Camera results expire after 500 ms in the service. The browser also clears its display after 1.5 seconds without a received frame.

Launch with pnpm install --frozen-lockfile, pnpm setup and pnpm dev. Model artifacts are pinned and loaded locally after setup. Viewer guide · Diagnostic architecture decision · Association details

Local development host was Apple Silicon with macOS 26.6.2. Model adapters ran on CPU. Browser screenshots and test fixtures contain no camera footage.

Buildpnpm build passed. The bundled viewer was 147.39 kB of JavaScript, 45.74 kB gzip, in the local build.
Real model smokeBoth adapters returned zero people on blank input and one person with 17 observed joints on the public MediaPipe example. On an in-memory three-copy composite, RTMPose returned three detections and MediaPipe returned two. The composite uses copies of one person. These are isolated model checks rather than installation accuracy or latency measurements.
