Two moles and a bomb

More time to reach each mole and three simultaneous targets give the player a choice of what to strike. The player can see each target’s remaining time in its own bar.

The default play area keeps two moles and one bomb active for the admitted player. Each strike or expiry replaces that target independently. Collisions use the existing Touch, Flick or Push gesture rules. A bomb strike costs 50 points, can take the score below zero and ends the current streak. Letting a bomb expire carries no penalty.

The game owns scoring and collision resolution. Separate limbs can strike different targets in the same frame. The game awards mole points before applying the bomb deduction. Pause and tracking interruptions freeze target lifetimes. Replay version 2 records the new rules and version 1 keeps its single-mole timing, scoring and output shape.
