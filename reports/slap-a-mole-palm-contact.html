Palm and foot contact

In Slap a Mole Touch, overlap a mole or bomb with a palm or foot ring. The visible ring and scoring use the same geometry.

Contact rules

The game checks whether the center distance is at most the sum of the target and limb radii. Each limb ring has radius 0.024 in normalized image coordinates. A foot ring is centered on the ankle.

The game now requests the installed MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite model. A palm center is halfway from the wrist to the midpoint of the index and pinky landmarks. All three points must be confident and in frame. A lost palm hides its ring. Scoring requires a fresh entry after reacquisition.

Replay v3 records the new Touch geometry. Versions 1 and 2 retain point-based Touch rules. The two-camera rehearsal selects v2 for its pointer controller. Camera Flick uses wrist movement.

Verification

  • pnpm check passed on Windows, including 752 TypeScript and 209 Python tests.
  • pnpm build built and verified all 7 mirror HTML entries.
  • In the browser, wrist-only overlap kept the score at 0 and hid the affected palm ring.
  • A palm center 0.012 normalized image units outside the target edge scored 100 when its ring overlapped the target.

On Windows CPU, the median inference time across 30 samples after five warm-ups was 13.86 ms for MediaPipe and 9.52 ms for RTMPose on an empty 960 × 540 image. Both detected zero people. These empty-frame timings exclude capture, player pose cost and display.

Physical palm accuracy, camera responsiveness and the ring size still need camera playtesting. Update the viewer and vision service together for the optional hand landmarks.
