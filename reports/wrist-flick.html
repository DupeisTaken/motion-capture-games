A quick flap.
A satisfying whack.

Mole Motion adds an optional Flick interaction: hold a wrist near a mole, make a short quick movement, then settle briefly before the next whack.

Issue #29 · PR link pending creation

What changed and why

Touch scores on a fresh target entry. Small flaps near a target and rapid motion between tracking samples need a different rule. Flick checks the recent wrist path against a small margin around the mole, with speed and travel thresholds to reject ordinary drift. The existing Touch and calibrated-depth Push rules stay available.

Either anatomical wrist can flick. Hand/wrist aliases share one limb history, with wrist preference when both are present. A miss, countdown flap or expired-target flap consumes the gesture; a held hand cannot score again. Missing limbs, source gaps, alias changes, pauses and invalid identity/framing/time reset history.

The source-only game owns detection and collision. No new camera, inference dependency, wire field, package or buffering queue was added. Replay version 1 supports the new interaction value; old touch/push replay behavior remains compatible. The CI component/build table remains accurate.

Image units are normalized upright, unmirrored camera coordinates, not meters. Defaults live together in TUNING.flick. Presentation applies the existing single mirror transform.
