Before this branch

Documentation defines two cameras and optional rig coordinates. There is no executable stereo reconstruction or measured geometry comparison.

After this branch

An isolated vision module triangulates named joints, computes the hip-midpoint root, and preserves source/calibration/clock metadata. Invalid timing, geometry or identity produces missing XYZ. Games and scores are outside this experiment.

Stand height: user-reported 1.4 m. The 2 m baseline, 1000 px focal length and 1920×1080 processed images below are synthetic assumptions, not validated 4K camera settings.
